Why PathLearn™

A fully encompassing training experience, not a pile of separate tools.

We designed PathLearn™ around how working professionals actually prepare. You learn with Expert-led modules, prove skills in labs, and check readiness with practice quizzes. Progress stays with you across phone and web.

01

Expert-led modules

Up-to-date training modules taught by Accumentum instructors. Video lessons resume where you left off so a commute or lunch break still moves the course forward.

02

Challenging lab environments

Hands-on labs built to match what the job and the exam expect. You practice accurate scenarios, not slide-only theory.

03

Practice quizzes for exam prep

Quiz questions with clear results so you see what you know and what still needs work before test day.

Inside the portal

Everything you need to train lives in PathLearn™.

Labs

Quizzes
